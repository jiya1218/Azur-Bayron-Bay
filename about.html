OUR STORY

A Relaxed Coastal Retreat

Azur Byron Bay is a boutique accommodation experience designed for those who value space, connection and the laid-back beauty of Byron Bay.

Property Layout

Azur Byron Bay features a main house and a separate rear house, offering a total of 11 bedrooms, 10 bathrooms and space for up to 6 cars. The layout includes spacious living areas, a pool and hot tub, tropical gardens and versatile accommodation options — perfect for families, couples and group getaways.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Balinese Warmth Meets Coastal Serenity

Drawing inspiration from Bali's open-air pavilions, natural timber finishes, and calming water gardens, Azur integrates handcrafted stone elements, antique Balinese carved doorway gates, and swaying native palms.

The result is a tranquil sanctuary where the boundaries between indoor relaxation and outdoor entertaining dissolve.
