WALKING DISTANCE & BEYOND

Byron Bay At Your Doorstep

Everything you could want in a coastal holiday is just a leisurely stroll away.

5 MIN WALK (450M)

Main Beach & Surf Club

Gentle rolling waves, golden sand, and panoramic views out to Julian Rocks and the Cape. Perfect for morning swims, surf lessons, or watching the sunset over Mount Warning.

Beach Guide ↗
5 MIN WALK (400M)

Byron Town & Culinary Scene

Wander past independent boutiques, organic juice bars, and award-winning dining including Light Years Asian Diner, Bang Bang, The Balcony Bar, and artisanal coffee roasters.

Dining Directory ↗
7 MIN DRIVE / SCENIC WALK

Cape Byron Lighthouse

Australia's most easterly point. Take the legendary coastal walking track past The Pass and Wategos Beach, and look out for resident pods of bottlenose dolphins and migrating humpback whales.
