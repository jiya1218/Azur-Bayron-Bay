GROUP BOOKINGS

Your Private Byron Bay Retreat

EXCLUSIVE ACCOMMODATION

Private Luxury for Up to 22 Guests

For milestone birthdays, family getaways, intimate celebrations or weekends away with friends, Azur Byron Bay offers a relaxed and private setting for bringing your favourite people together.

With exclusive use of the property, Azur can accommodate up to 22 guests, giving your group the freedom to settle in, slow down and enjoy Byron Bay entirely at your own pace.

Spacious accommodation, tropical gardens, shared entertaining areas, a swimming pool and hot tub create an effortless setting for long lunches, late conversations and memorable days spent together. All just a short walk from Byron Bay's town centre and Main Beach.

DESIGNED FOR GROUPS

Made for Getting Together

Some occasions deserve more than separate hotel rooms.

At Azur, your group can stay together while still having the space to retreat when you need it. From couples and families to groups of friends, the property has been designed around relaxed shared living, with plenty of room to gather indoors and out.
