OUR STORY

A Relaxed Coastal Retreat

Azur Byron Bay is a boutique accommodation experience designed for those who value space, connection and the laid-back beauty of Byron Bay.

Just five minutes from the town centre and Main Beach, our thoughtfully curated beach houses and suites offer the perfect balance of relaxed luxury and home away from home comfort, with a subtle Balinese influence woven throughout.
