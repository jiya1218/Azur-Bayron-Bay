Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding check-in, parking, pool hours, and staying with us.

What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged subject to availability with advance notice.
Is parking provided on site? +
Yes, Azur Byron Bay has private, off-street parking bays for up to 6 vehicles with easy access via Shirley Lane.
How close is Azur to the beach and town centre? +
Azur is situated at 44 Shirley Street, a gentle five-minute flat walk (approx. 400m) to Byron Bay's town center, shops, supermarkets, and Main Beach. You will not need a car once you arrive!
Can we book the entire property exclusively? +
Yes! Azur Byron Bay specializes in whole-estate group bookings accommodating up to 22 guests across 11 bedrooms and 10 bathrooms with exclusive access to the swimming pool, heated hot tub, and tropical entertaining areas.
What are the swimming pool and hot tub hours? +
The swimming pool and heated spa are open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM for all in-house guests.
